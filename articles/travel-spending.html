TRAVEL BASICS · 5 MIN READ

Make a simple travel spending plan

Start with fixed costs: transport, accommodation, document fees and any bookings already paid. Then estimate daily costs for food, transport, mobile data and small purchases.

Remember the first-day costs that are easy to forget: airport transport, a SIM, an adapter or an accommodation deposit. Keep an emergency amount separate from your expected spending.

Record real spending during the first few days and adjust the plan. This is general planning information, not financial advice.
