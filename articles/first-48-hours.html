Your first 48 hours in a new country

Focus first on safety, communication and secure access to your documents. Save your accommodation address offline, use transport you can identify and let a trusted person know you arrived safely.

Arrange a local SIM or secure data connection, find essential shops and learn the route back to your accommodation in daylight. Keep passport originals separate from copies and check official government sources for any arrival registration requirement.

Do not try to complete everything on day one. Rest, charge devices, make a small plan for the next day and verify important information through official channels.
